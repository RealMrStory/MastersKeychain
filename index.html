Keychain Maker

Turn your drawing into a colour keychain you can 3D print.

1Pick and crop your drawing

Drag a box around your drawing to cut out the desk and edges. Drag a corner to fix the box. Dark marker on white paper works best.

2Choose a style

3Fix up your lines

4Colour it in

Pick one of your 4 colours, then click a part of your keychain in the Top view to fill it. Lines can be filled too.

Change this colour to
Colour of the back

Your picture is the top 1 mm. Everything behind it prints in the back colour.

Colours 1 to 4 are filament slots 1 to 4 on the printer.

5Set the size

6Add the hole

Press Move hole, then click or drag in the Top view. Put it inside your drawing, or just outside and it grows its own loop.

7Name it
